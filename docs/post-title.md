# Add a title to your page

Every PageRush page needs a title. Copy either option below and replace `Your page title` with your title.

## Option 1: title at the top

Copy this at the very top of your content:

```yaml
---
title: "Your page title"
---
```

## Option 2: title as the first line

Copy this as the first line of your content:

```markdown
# Your page title
```

Use only one of these options. Then try publishing again.
